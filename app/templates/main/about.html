What is RaceLink?

RaceLink is a web-based platform that makes marathon registration simple and convenient. It allows runners to register online, stay updated on upcoming races, and manage their participation easily. Organizers can also create, manage, and track events and payments through the system.

Developed by four Computer Science students from the University of the East, RaceLink aims to digitalize marathon bookings and connect the running community in one easy-to-use platform.
